# Shotlist — "De China a México: lo que de verdad cuesta importar"

**Logline:** a Mexican importer sees USD 10,000 on a Chinese proforma and learns, in 60 seconds,
why it lands at ~USD 15,945, and what to check before paying.

Format 1080×1920 (9:16) · 60.0 s · 120 BPM (beat 0.5 s, bar 2 s, 30 bars) · on-screen language es-MX.
Every shot starts on a bar line. Times are global seconds. SFX names refer to `audio/score.py`.

Worked example (illustrative, USD), reused in the hook, proforma and cost stack:

| Line | Calc | USD |
|---|---|---|
| Mercancía (FOB) | 500 pzas × 20.00 | 10,000 |
| Flete + seguro | ocean LCL, door | 1,200 |
| Valor en aduana | mercancía + flete + seguro | 11,200 |
| IGI (arancel) | ej. 15 % × 11,200 | 1,680 |
| DTA | ~0.8 % × 11,200 | 90 |
| IVA | 16 % × (11,200 + 1,680 + 90) | 2,075 |
| Agente + puerto + flete local | ~ | 900 |
| **Costo total** | | **15,945 (≈1.6×)** |
